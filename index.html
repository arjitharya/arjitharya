About

A little about my story.

I'm an Undergraduate student at the University of Hong Kong studying Computer Engineering, having finished high school at UIA International School of Tokyo. I started out by just building websites for fun, then got serious once I interned with a ML R&D team, building passion projects and messing with circuits and digital logic in my spare time.

I like projects where code turns into something physical — building a computer out of NAND gates, or getting an actuator to react to sensor input. That's mostly why I've ended up focused on robotics: it's where software and hardware meet to solve real world problems.

Right now I'm working toward EVA (Empathic Virtual Assistant) — think of it as an Alexa built specifically to offer emotional support in elderly care homes. HumorBot was my first prototype toward that idea, and I'm continuing to build it out as a longer-term passion project.
